## Discussion Topics

### Topic 1: Donation of Docling Graph project

**Summary:**  

IBM is donating the Docling Graph project to the LF AI & Data which will be governed as a Docling satellite project.
- IBM finalized the legal document with the LF
- The TAC of LF AI & Data will vote on this in the meeting on Feb 12 2026.
- The Docling TSC has to approve this as well.

References:
- [Docling Graph GitHub repository](https://github.com/IBM/docling-graph/)
- [Docling governance](https://github.com/docling-project/community/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md#satellite-projects)


**Decisions:**
- Approved.
